# Tai Chi narration sources

Original Chinese lesson overview text: this project, adapted from the installed Tai Chi course instructions. Course teaching references remain in the course/media manifests.

Locally synthesized with the official public Qwen/Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-1.7B-CustomVoice, revision `0c0e3051f131929182e2c023b9537f8b1c68adfe`, stock Serena voice. Qwen3-TTS code/model license: Apache-2.0; license copied in license-qwen.txt.

The same speaker, Chinese language, instruction, seed and sampler were used as the user's previously accepted Baduanjin voice profile. No personal voice was cloned. No time stretching, pitch shifting, silence trimming or EQ was applied. Only PCM assets go to KV260; neural synthesis runs locally on the PC.

This source record is not human acceptance of all new clips or professional validation of Tai Chi technique.
